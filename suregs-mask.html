Süreg's Mask

June / 10 / 2024

Süreg's Mask is a team project developed for the “Project II” subject at CITM University, as part of the Video Games Design & Development and Design, Animation, and Digital Art degrees.

It’s a 2D isometric RPG where you must descend through eight dungeon floors to uncover the mysterious force that calls to you, learn the hidden story of your late father, and obtain four unique masks with powerful abilities.

In Süreg's Mask, you can fight monsters in the dungeons, upgrade your weapons at the blacksmith, sell items dropped by beasts, buy potions, enhance your mask abilities through a mystical skill tree, and even go fishing.

The game also includes various menus such as an inventory to view item details, a journal to read notes left behind in the dungeons, and an enemy compendium containing detailed information about all discovered foes.

As the Audio Programmer, I was responsible for sourcing and implementing all in-game audio, ensuring every sound contributed to an immersive and cohesive experience.
