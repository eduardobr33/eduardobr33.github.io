Hired or Fired

June / 3 / 2025

Hired or Fired is an immersive VR experience where you take on the role of a Mafia boss running business from your office. Your mission? Decide who’s trustworthy enough to join your criminal empire — and who deserves to be fired… literally.

Interview shady characters, examine their documents and photos, and interrogate them using voice commands or scripted dialogue. Then make your judgment: pay them… or punish them.

During this project, I was responsible for implementing all NPC logic, animations, sounds, and the game systems related to NPC interactions. My focus was on creating believable and responsive AI behavior to make the experience truly immersive.
